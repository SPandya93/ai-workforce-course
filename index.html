AI Research & Intelligence Brief

Investigate an emerging workforce, business, technology, or industry trend—and check the evidence before recommending action.

  • Evaluate accuracy, credibility, and reliability.
  • Verify key findings with credible sources.

Activity 3
10 POINTS · 10% OF COURSE GRADE

AI Workflow & Productivity Optimization

Identify a task or process that could work better. Analyze the current workflow and propose an AI-enhanced solution.

What you’ll practice

  • Identify inefficiencies and implementation challenges.
  • Match AI support to an actual workplace need.
  • Explain benefits and limitations of your proposed solution.

What you’ll present

Your proposed workflow and its expected contribution to productivity, efficiency, decision-making, quality, or customer experience.

Activity 4
10 POINTS · 10% OF COURSE GRADE

AI Image Replication Challenge

Recreate an instructor-selected image through multiple rounds of prompt refinement. Learn how clarity, specificity, and structure influence the result.

What you’ll practice

  • Describe composition, objects, color, lighting, and style.
  • Compare outputs and revise your instructions.
  • Explain what changed and why it helped.

What you’ll submit

Your final image, prompt iterations, and a brief reflection on your prompting strategy and lessons learned.

A few things to know

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I submit my work and check grades?

Submit assignments through Canvas. Use Canvas for announcements, detailed assignment instructions, feedback, and grades. This website is a course companion and does not accept submissions or record grades.

What textbook do I need?

The required textbook is Tom Taulli’s Artificial Intelligence Basics: A Non-Technical Introduction (2019), Apress, ISBN 978-1484250273. Check the syllabus and the JSU V.I.B.E. program for information about accessing course materials.

Does the course meet online or in person?

The listed class time is Tuesday and Thursday, 12:00–1:20 PM, in COE 304. The syllabus schedule identifies alternating online and face-to-face weeks, with additional face-to-face meetings near the end of the semester. Follow current Canvas announcements for the latest meeting arrangements.

What format should written assignments follow?

The syllabus requires APA 7 style, double spacing, 12-point Times New Roman, one-inch margins, page numbers, and an APA 7 cover page. Follow the requirements for each assignment in Canvas.

What is the late work policy?

Late work cannot earn a perfect score; the maximum begins at a B or lower depending on lateness. The acceptance deadline is 5:00 PM two days after the original due date. Read the syllabus and Canvas instructions for the full requirements.

Can I propose my own innovation project?

Yes. The student-proposed pathway must align with course learning objectives and demonstrate equivalent rigor, creativity, innovation, and practical application. You must receive instructor approval before beginning.

How do I get help with Canvas?

For 24/7 Canvas assistance, call 1-844-358-8765 or use the Help icon in Canvas. For JSU support Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM–5:00 PM, email canvas@jsums.edu or call 601-979-0245.

Does the practice prompt builder save or send my text?

No. The builder assembles text in the current browser page without contacting an AI model or saving your entries. Copy your prompt before leaving the page, and use fictional or de-identified information for practice.
